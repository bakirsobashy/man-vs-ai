## Privacy Policy for Man vs AI

**Last updated:** [date]

[Studio or developer name] ("we") makes the mobile game **Man vs AI** (the "game"). This policy explains what
information the game uses and why.

### Information stored on your device
The game saves your progress, settings and the list of cosmetic items you own on your own device. That includes
save slots, run progress, audio and control settings, language, and owned cosmetics. We don't receive this
information. It stays on your device until you delete a save slot in the game or uninstall the game.

The game has no accounts and no chat. It doesn't ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.

### Advertising
The game can show **optional rewarded ads**. You choose to watch them, for example for a second chance after a defeat
or a bonus after a run. The ads are served by **Google AdMob**. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's
software in the game may collect:
- your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
- your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location;
- information about how you interact with ads and the app;
- diagnostic information.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the game asks for your consent
before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Privacy options**, which appears
where required. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.

Learn how Google uses this information:
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

### Purchases
Optional cosmetic purchases are processed by **Google Play**. We don't receive your payment details. The game
receives only confirmation of which items you own, so it can unlock them and restore them later. See the
[Google Play Terms of Service](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/) and Google's privacy policy.

### Children
The game isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

### Sharing and selling
We don't sell personal information. We don't share it except through the Google services described above, which
process it under their own policies.

### Security and retention
The game stores its data in its private storage on your device. Google's services send their data over encrypted
connections. Google keeps that data according to its own retention policies.

### Changes
If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date.
