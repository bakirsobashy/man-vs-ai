# Store listing (draft)

Play Console → Grow → Store presence → Main store listing. The title is still the working one (Q3 in
[open-questions.md](../open-questions.md)): check trademarks and store-name availability before launch.

Play's metadata policy: no references to other games or apps, no ranking claims ("#1", "best"), no prices or
promotions in the title, and no excessive emoji or capitals.

## Texts
**App name** (30 characters max):
> Man vs AI

**Short description** (80 characters max; this one is 68):
> One-finger roguelite shooter. Every upgrade costs a piece of the man.

**Full description** (4,000 characters max):
> The machines took his family. Now Sergeant Kade is going to take the city back, one district at a time, even if he
> has to become a machine to do it.
>
> **Man vs AI** is a fast top-down bullet-hell roguelite built for one finger in portrait mode. Drag to move: Kade
> aims and fires on his own. Flick to roll through bullet storms. Lift your finger near cover to dive behind it.
> Double-tap to fire an EMP that wipes the screen.
>
> **Every upgrade costs a piece of you**
> Dr. Vorn can rebuild Kade with eyes that lock on from farther away, arms that reload faster, and a heart that takes
> more punishment.
> Each implant makes you stronger and changes Kade for good. How much of the man is left at the end decides which
> ending you get.
>
> **Features**
> • One-finger controls designed for phones: move, shoot, roll, take cover and EMP without a second thumb
> • Five districts, from the Rustline Slums to the Syndicate Core, each with new robots and hazards
> • Five boss fights against the Syndicate's lieutenants, each in three phases
> • 21 guns to find and unlock, from scrap cannons to beam cutters
> • Six body slots and 18 implant upgrades that change how you play and how the story ends
> • Memories of Kade's family hidden in every level, and endings that reflect the choices you made
> • Threat levels after you finish, for harder runs with bigger rewards
> • Three save slots, so you can play a different life in each
>
> **Fair to play**
> • No energy timers. Play as long as you like.
> • Ads are always optional: watch one for a second chance or a bonus, or don't.
> • Purchases are cosmetic only: outfits, weapon skins, lab decorations and EMP styles. Nothing sold changes the game.
> • Plays offline.
>
> **Accessibility**
> Color-blind and high-contrast bullet palettes, larger text, reduced flashing, adjustable joystick and swipe
> sensitivity, and a 30 fps battery saver.

Check "Plays offline" on a device with ads and purchases installed. Ads and purchases need the network, but the
game itself never does.
